## Fact check

**Populations (UN World Population Prospects 2024, estimates for 2025, rounded):**

| Rank | Country | People | Area (M km²) | Resize factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | India | 1.46B | 3.29 | ×2.7 |
| 2 | China | 1.42B | 9.60 | ×1.6 |
| 3 | USA | 347M | 9.83 | ÷1.3 |
| 4 | Indonesia | 286M | 1.90 | ×1.6 |
| 5 | Pakistan | 255M | 0.88 | ×2.2 |
| 6 | Nigeria | 238M | 0.92 | ×2.1 |
| 7 | Brazil | 213M | 8.51 | ÷1.6 |
| 8 | Bangladesh | 176M | 0.15 | ×4.4 |
| 9 | Russia | 144M | 17.10 | ÷2.7 |
| 10 | Ethiopia | 135M | 1.10 | ×1.4 |

**How the resize works:** the factor is the square root of the country's population density divided by the world average (about 61 people per km² of land). It scales width and height, so the redrawn *area* is proportional to population.

**Claims in the video:**
- **India + China:** 2.88 billion of about 8.2 billion people, which is 35%, more than a third.
