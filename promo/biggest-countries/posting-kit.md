## 12. Fact check

**Areas (total, CIA World Factbook / UN):**

| Rank | Country | Area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Russia | 17.10M km² |
| 2 | Canada | 9.98M km² |
| 3 | USA | 9.83M km² |
| 4 | China | 9.60M km² |
| 5 | Brazil | 8.51M km² |
| 6 | Australia | 7.69M km² |
| 7 | India | 3.29M km² |
| 8 | Argentina | 2.78M km² |
| 9 | Kazakhstan | 2.72M km² |
| 10 | Algeria | 2.38M km² |

**Claims in the video:**
- **Russia:** 17.1M of Earth's 148.9M km² of land is about 11.5%. It has 11 time zones and is 1.74× the USA ("almost twice").
- **Brazil:** the EU is 4.23M km², so Brazil is 2.0× the EU. It covers 47.7% of South America and is bigger than the contiguous USA (about 8.08M km²).
- **Alaska:** 1.72M km² total, more than Texas + California + Montana (1.50M km²). It would rank about 17th among countries.
- **Canada:** coastline of 243,042 km, the longest of any country. It holds about 62% of the world's lakes larger than 10 hectares (HydroLAKES).
- **China:** spans five geographic time zones but uses one official time (UTC+8).
- **Australia:** 7.69M ÷ Texas 0.696M ≈ 11.
- **India:** 3.29M km² is one third of the USA. Population about 1.45 billion vs about 340 million (4.3×).
- **Argentina:** about 3,650 km north to south. 2.78M ÷ UK 0.244M ≈ 11.4.
- **Kazakhstan:** the largest landlocked country. 2.72M ÷ Japan 0.378M ≈ 7.2.
- **Algeria:** the largest country in Africa. 2.38M ÷ metropolitan France 0.55M ≈ 4.3. Over 80% is Sahara.

**About the shapes:** every "true size" shot uses equal-area projections at one shared scale per shot. The repeated shapes (such as 11 Texases) overlap for display; the count is by area.
