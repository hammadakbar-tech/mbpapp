## 8. Ready replies

- **"China is bigger than the USA!"** → *It depends how you count! By total area (CIA/UN) the USA is 9.83M km² and China 9.60M. By land only, China wins.*
- **"Where's Greenland?"** → *Greenland (2.17M km²) is part of the Kingdom of Denmark, not an independent country. It would be #12!*
- **"Where's Antarctica?"** → *14.2M km², so it would be #2… but it's a continent with no government.*
- **"Russia looks way bigger on maps"** → *That's the Mercator projection stretching things near the poles. These are equal-area shapes.*
- **"Do the smallest countries!"** → *Already on the channel! 🔍*
