**How it plays:**

**Fast hook (0:00–0:12).** Four hard cuts, each with a hit on the punch word:
1. "An emperor, buried with **8,000 clay soldiers**": a terracotta army rises row by row while a counter races to 8,000.
2. "A wall, **over 20,000 km** long": the Great Wall draws itself across the map while the km counter runs to 21,196.
3. "Paper. Gunpowder. The compass.": three cards slam in, then the stamp "ALL INVENTED IN CHINA".
4. "A civilisation that has lasted **3,000+ years**": 13 dynasties flash by as seal stamps while the year spins back to 1600 BC.

Then the title slam.

**Five chapters.** Real dynasty borders change on the narrator's words, and a seal-stamp plate shows each dynasty's name and years:
- Shang oracle bones, the Mandate of Heaven, Confucius
- the Qin conquest arrows and the terracotta army
- the Silk Road, the Grand Canal, the Tang golden age and the Song inventions
- the Mongols, the Forbidden City and Great Wall, and Zheng He's fleet to Africa
- the Qing, the Opium Wars, Puyi's cracked crown ("2,132 YEARS OF EMPERORS… OVER"), 1949, and today

**Ending:** "From writing on bones… to the far side of the Moon", then "Which dynasty was the greatest?" with five dynasty seals to vote on. An easy, opinionated comment.

## Fact check
- **Terracotta Army:**
  - About 8,000 soldiers, plus roughly 130 chariots and 670 horses, buried in pits beside the tomb of Qin Shi Huang near Xi'an.
  - Discovered in 1974. "8,000" is the standard estimate.
- **Great Wall:**
  - China's 2012 national survey measured all walls from all dynasties at **21,196 km**.
  - The line on the map is a simplified route of the Ming wall.
- **Inventions:**
  - Paper: Han dynasty (reported by Cai Lun in 105 AD; earlier examples exist).
  - Gunpowder: discovered by Tang-era alchemists (9th century).
  - Compass: the lodestone "south-pointer" dates to the Han; the needle compass was used for navigation by the Song (records from 1088 and 1117).
- **3,000+ years:** oracle bone script dates from about 1250 BC, so China has about 3,270 years of written history.
- **Shang (c. 1600–1046 BC):**
  - Its late capital, Yin, was near modern Anyang.
  - Oracle bones (about 1250–1050 BC) carry the earliest known Chinese writing and were used for divination.
  - The Shang extent on the map is approximate.
- **Zhou (1046–256 BC):** the Mandate of Heaven justified the Zhou overthrow of the Shang.
- **Confucius (551–479 BC):** lived in the Spring and Autumn period, before the Warring States (475–221 BC).
- **Qin:**
  - Unified China in 221 BC. Qin Shi Huang ruled as emperor from 221 to 210 BC.
  - He standardised the script, the currency (the "ban liang" coin, with its square hole) and weights and measures, and linked earlier walls.
  - The Qin fell in 206 BC, four years after his death.
- **Han (202 BC – 220 AD):**
  - Opened the Silk Road (Zhang Qian's missions, about 138–126 BC).
  - Han Chinese are about 91% of China's population (2020 census).
- **Division (220–589):** the Three Kingdoms, Jin, and Northern and Southern dynasties, about 369 years.
- **Sui (581–618):**
  - Reunified China in 589 and built the Grand Canal (main works 605–611).
  - The Beijing–Hangzhou Grand Canal (about 1,776 km) is widely cited as the longest canal in the world (UNESCO World Heritage site since 2014).
- **Tang (618–907):** Chang'an is estimated at about 1 million people, one of the largest cities in the world at the time.
- **Song (960–1279):**
  - Bi Sheng's movable type (1040s).
  - The first government-issued paper money (jiaozi, 1024).
  - The compass used for navigation.
  - The earliest known gunpowder formula (Wujing Zongyao, 1044) and the first gunpowder weapons.
- **Yuan:**
  - Kublai Khan, grandson of Genghis Khan, ruled 1260–1294 and proclaimed the Yuan in 1271.
  - The conquest of the Song was complete in 1279. Khanbaliq is today's Beijing.
- **Ming (1368–1644):**
  - Founded by Zhu Yuanzhang, born to a poor peasant family.
  - The Forbidden City was built 1406–1420.
  - Most of the Great Wall that survives today is Ming.
- **Zheng He:** made seven voyages (1405–1433) and reached East Africa (Malindi, Mogadishu). Columbus sailed in 1492.
- **Qing (1644–1912):**
  - The Manchus took Beijing in 1644.
  - Peak area was about 13 million km² (around 1790; estimates range from about 13 to 14.7 million).
- **Opium Wars:**
  - Fought in 1839–42 and 1856–60; Britain won both, with France joining the second.
  - Hong Kong was ceded in 1842.
  - "Century of Humiliation" (1839–1949) is the term used in China.
- **Puyi:**
  - Born 7 February 1906. His abdication was announced on 12 February 1912, when he was 6.
  - 221 BC to 1912 is 2,132 years of emperors.
- **War:**
  - The civil war between the Nationalists and the Communists ran from 1927 to 1949, with a pause during the war against Japan.
  - Japan invaded Manchuria in 1931, and full-scale war ran from 1937 to 1945.
- **1949:** Mao Zedong (1893–1976) proclaimed the People's Republic on 1 October 1949. The Nationalist (ROC) government retreated to Taiwan.
- **Today:**
  - Population about 1.41 billion (2024).
  - The world's second-largest economy by nominal GDP.
- **Moon:** Chang'e 4 made the first ever soft landing on the far side of the Moon, on 3 January 2019.
- **Maps:**
  - Dynasty extents come from the historical-basemaps dataset (approximate, shown at one moment in each dynasty).
  - Modern borders come from Natural Earth. Taiwan is shown separately.
