## What it is

An original second-person suspense story in flat 2D animation. The story, characters, script, art and sound were all made for this video.

**Hook (0:00–0:05):** "The baby monitor says the baby is asleep in his crib. But you're holding the baby."

**Story beats:**
1. First night babysitting Noah for the Carters. He's asleep on your shoulder.
2. The nursery camera shows a small shape breathing under the crib blanket.
3. You text Mrs Carter: "is there a second baby??" She replies: "No. Just Noah. Why?"
4. The app pings: "Motion detected. Nursery."
5. The blanket slides down. The shape sits up, far too tall to be a baby. The "IN THE HOUSE" counter flips from 2 to 3.
6. It turns its head to the camera. "CAMERA OFFLINE."
7. A floorboard creaks upstairs. The phone's speaker crackles.
8. A whisper in Mrs Carter's voice: "bring him up, sweetie. It's bedtime."
9. A text from the real Mrs Carter: still at dinner, 20 minutes away.
10. The nursery door opens. Footsteps stop at the top of the stairs.
11. Noah lifts his head, looks up into the dark… and smiles.

**Ending:** "Go upstairs… or leave? Comment one word. The majority writes Part 2."

## Format features

- A HUD on every shot: the clock (11:52 → 11:55 PM), "BABYSITTING: NOAH", an "IN THE HOUSE" counter, and "PARENTS: 20 MIN AWAY".
- A new camera angle every 2–5 seconds: phone close-ups, the living room, the baby, the staircase, slow push-ins and tilts.
- Sound design: a low drone, a heartbeat that speeds up, a notification ping, static, floor creaks, footsteps, a phone buzz.
- No blood or gore, so it stays safe for monetisation.
