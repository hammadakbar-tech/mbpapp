  - The driest inhabited continent, ~70% arid, with the deserts named.

- **Today:**

  - Perth is closer to Bali than to Sydney.

## Fact check
- **Size:**
  - Australia is about 4,000 km wide; the Moon's diameter is 3,474 km.
  - Australia's area is 7.69 million km²; the contiguous USA is about 8.08 million km² ("almost as big").
- **Population:** Australia about 27.5 million (ABS 2025); Texas about 31 million; USA about 340 million.
- **The coast:**
  - About 85% live within 50 km of the coast (ABS).
  - About two-thirds live in the 8 Greater Capital City areas.
- **Climate:**
  - Australia is the driest inhabited continent.
  - About 70% of it is arid or semi-arid (Bureau of Meteorology).
  - The record is 50.7 °C, at Oodnadatta, SA (2 Jan 1960), tied by Onslow, WA (13 Jan 2022).
- **Northern Territory:**
  - About 1.35 million km², larger than France and Spain combined (about 1.06 million km²).
  - Population about 250,000.
- **First Nations:** at least 50,000 years of continuous presence; some evidence suggests 65,000.
- **Colonies:**
  - Sydney Cove 1788, Hobart 1804, Brisbane 1824, Perth (Swan River) 1829, Melbourne 1835, Adelaide 1836.
  - The narration lists these in a different order for flow. The on-screen pins show the correct years.
- **Sheep:** about 78 million sheep (ABARES 2023–24) vs about 27 million people, so about 3 per person.
- **Cities:**
  - Greater Sydney about 5.5M, Greater Melbourne about 5.2M; together about 39%.
  - Perth–Bali is about 2,600 km; Perth–Sydney is about 3,300 km.
- **Map:** the arid-zone outline and Great Dividing Range line are approximate. The USA, Texas, France and Spain are shown at true size (equal-area projection).
