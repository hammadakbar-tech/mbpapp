## Fact notes
- **Sweden:** 267,570 islands, the most of any country (Statistics Sweden 2013).
- **Swiss chocolate:** ~11 kg per person per year (Chocosuisse, approximate).
- **Norway:** coastline 25,148 km (CIA, mainland); GDP per person ~US$89K vs Philippines ~US$4.3K (IMF, approximate).
- **Belgium:** coastline 66.5 km (CIA).
- **Denmark:** coastline 7,314 km (CIA). The Dannebrog's 1219 date is a legend; it's widely cited as the oldest national flag still in use (Guinness).
- **Finland:** 187,888 lakes (Finnish Environment Institute); ~3 million saunas (estimate).
- **Prague Castle:** the largest ancient castle complex (Guinness). **Sugar cubes:** Jakub Kryštof Rad, 1843.
- **Rubik's Cube:** Ernő Rubik, 1974. **Lake Hévíz:** the largest swimmable thermal lake.
- **Croatia:** 1,246 islands, islets and rocks; coastline 5,835 km (CIA); 2018 World Cup runner-up.
- **Mountains:** Dufourspitze 4,634 m; Grossglockner 3,798 m; Mount Apo 2,954 m.
