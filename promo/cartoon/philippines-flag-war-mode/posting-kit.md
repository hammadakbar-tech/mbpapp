## Fact notes
- **Republic Act No. 8491** (Flag and Heraldic Code of the Philippines, 1998): the flag is displayed with the **red field on top in time of war**, and blue on top in peacetime.
- **Colours:** blue = peace, truth and justice; red = patriotism and valour (the official symbolism). White = equality.
- **3 stars:** Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao. **8 sun rays:** the first 8 provinces that revolted against Spain (Manila, Cavite, Bulacan, Pampanga, Tarlac, Laguna, Batangas and Nueva Ecija).
- **Japan's flag:** a centred circle, so it looks the same when rotated.
