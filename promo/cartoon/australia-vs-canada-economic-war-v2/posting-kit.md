## Ready replies

- **"Canada's minimum wage is higher in my province!"** → True. The video uses the federal rate (C$18.15). Provinces range from C$15 (Alberta) to C$19.75 (Nunavut). Australia's national rate is A$26.44.
- **"Exchange rates change!"** → Yes, US-dollar values are approximate. At recent rates Australia still pays more per hour.
- **"Housing is bad in both"** → Exactly why Canada "wins" with "still terrible, just less terrible" 😅 Sydney is #2 least affordable in the world, Vancouver about #5.
- **"Australia had a recession in 2020"** → Yes, COVID ended the run. That's why it's "almost 30 years without a recession" (1991–2020).
- **"Canada has more oil than that"** → The ~170 billion barrels is proven reserves, mostly oil sands. Among the largest on Earth.
- **"Why version 2?"** → V1 was the geography wars. V2 is the money edition 💰 Who should face off next?
- **"Rigged!"** → It was 4–4 until the gold 🥇
