# Countryballs: Australia + Canada vs Russia + India, the Tag Team War

**File:** `tag-team-war.mp4` (1080×1920, 2:08, AI voices)
**Cover:** `cover.png`

A new 2v2 format. It ends in a **4–4 tie on purpose**: nobody loses, and viewers are asked to pick the tiebreaker in the comments and subscribe for the decider (part 2). It sticks to geography and fun facts, with nothing political. Russia and India are shown with flag-balls and charts instead of map outlines, to avoid border disputes.

## How it plays

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cold open | Australia: "Canada! Team-up, mate?" 🤝 Canada: "Sorry… against who, eh?" Russia (snowy onion domes): "Against the biggest country on Earth." India: "And the most people on Earth!" |
| 0:08 | Title | Four balls slide in: AUS + CAN vs RUS + IND, "THE TAG TEAM WAR" |
| 0:16 | Round 1: Size | Area squares: 17.7M vs 20.4M km² → **RUS + IND** |
| 0:27 | Round 2: People | 7 figures vs 160 (each = 10 million): 70M vs 1.6B → **RUS + IND** |
| 0:39 | Round 3: Coastline | Russia ~37,650 km (Arctic). Canada's coastline traced: 243,042 km, + Australia ≈ 300,000 → **AUS + CAN** |
| 0:51 | Round 4: Cold | Snag, Yukon −63°C vs Oymyakon −67.7°C, "people live there, every winter" → **RUS + IND** |
| 1:04 | Round 5: Money per person | India's growth rocket. Then bars ~$70K / $58K vs ~$17K / ~$3K → **AUS + CAN** |
| 1:18 | Round 6: Cricket | India's 2 World Cups (1983, 2011) vs Australia's 6 → **AUS + CAN** |
| 1:27 | Round 7: Space | Canadarm (1981) vs first satellite (1957), first human (1961), first landing near the Moon's south pole (India, 2023) → **RUS + IND** |
| 1:40 | Round 8: Living longer | 83.2 / 81.7 vs 72.8 / 72.0 → **AUS + CAN** |
| 1:52 | The tie | Australia: "Four all?! Nobody wins?" India: "Then let the viewers decide!" |
| 1:58 | CTA | IT'S A TIE! 4–4. "YOU PICK THE TIEBREAKER": 🔥 heat? ⚽ football? 🍛 food? Comment it, then the subscribe button gets clicked |

**Part 2 tip:** the heat round is a ready-made decider. India's record is 51.0°C (Phalodi, 2016), just above Australia's 50.7°C. If viewers suggest heat, that's a twist ending.
