## Ready replies

- **"Where is France / Austria / Switzerland / Czechia?"** → In the middle, on purpose! France is the twist at the end. Where would you put it? 😄
- **"Poland isn't North Europe"** → Fair point. We split by latitude, roughly the Alps and the Danube. Poland ended up north of the line. North or Central for you?
- **"Luxembourg isn't the richest, Monaco/Liechtenstein is"** → In IMF data, Luxembourg is #1. Monaco and Liechtenstein aren't in the IMF tables. In World Bank data they come out higher.
- **"Spain beat Argentina, not Italy!"** → Yes, Spain won the 2026 final against Argentina. "Second" means Spain's second World Cup after 2010.
- **"England has 1, it's coming home"** → Still counted! Germany 4 + England 1 = 5 for the north. The south still has 6. ⚽
- **"Coffee was invented in Italy?"** → Coffee came from Ethiopia and Yemen. Italy gave the world espresso, and Finland drinks twice as much. ☕
- **"Rigged!"** → It ended 4–4. Which round would you flip?
- **"Do East vs West next"** → Noted 👀 Which countries do you want in it?

## Notes

- North/South are a meme split, not an official definition. The middle group (France, Switzerland, Austria, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Moldova, Ukraine, Belarus) is shown grey and left out of the score.
