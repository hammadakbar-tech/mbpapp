## 3. Thumbnail

Upload a custom thumbnail rather than an auto-picked frame:
- **Best for Facebook:** `thumbnails/thumb-C-6ties.png`. The curiosity hook matches the post text ("6 TIES?!").

### Manual ad-break times (at the cards between episodes)

These come just before each episode starts. Facebook needs the first break after 1:00 and breaks at least 2 minutes apart:
```
2:21, 4:52, 7:20, 9:32, 11:42, 13:54, 15:15, 17:27, 19:30, 21:32, 23:35
```
If Facebook limits how many you can use, choose **every second one** (2:21, 7:20, 11:42, 15:15, 19:30, 23:35).

## 6. When to post

Your audience is mostly Australian. On Facebook the evening scroll peaks around 7–9 pm.
- **Best:** Friday, Saturday or Sunday at **7:30 pm Sydney time (AEDT)**, which is **08:30 UTC**.
- Post **1 day after** the YouTube premiere, so the two platforms don't compete for the same fans on day one.

## 7. Push traffic from your Reels

Facebook Reels can't link to a Page video directly, but you can do this:
1. Post your existing 9:16 episodes as **Reels**, with the caption ending: "🎬 Watch the FULL Season 1 on our Page (26 min)!"
2. Comment on each Reel with the **Facebook video link** (Facebook allows its own links) and pin it.
3. **Share** the long video to your **Story** on publish day with a "WATCH" sticker.
