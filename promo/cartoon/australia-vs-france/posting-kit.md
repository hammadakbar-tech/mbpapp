# Countryballs: Australia vs France, the Geography War

**File:** `australia-vs-france.mp4` (1080×1920, 2:24, AI voices)
**Cover:** `cover.png`

Multi-shot editing like Canada vs Japan and Australia vs Japan: close-ups, low angles, over-the-shoulder and two-shots, with cutaways to whatever is being talked about and a reaction shot after each round. France talks in front of a Paris sunset with an Eiffel Tower silhouette, rooftops and the Seine.

**The hook twist:** France is secretly Australia's neighbour, through New Caledonia. It's teased at 0:05, revealed in round 6, and paid off in the ending ("Wait… France is my neighbour?!").

## Ready replies

- **"France isn't Australia's neighbour!"** → New Caledonia is part of France, about 1,500 km from Brisbane. Closer to Australia than Perth is to Sydney! 🇫🇷🇦🇺
- **"Why 12 time zones?"** → Overseas territories: Tahiti, Marquesas, Gambier, Clipperton, Guadeloupe/Martinique, Guiana/St-Pierre, Paris, Mayotte, Réunion, Kerguelen, New Caledonia, Wallis & Futuna. 13 with the Antarctic claim.
- **"Australia has more time zones"** → With all its external territories it has a few more, but France still has the most of any country.
- **"Michelin isn't fair"** → True, Michelin simply doesn't publish an Australian guide. Australia has amazing food. The round was about stars 😅
- **"Australia's sea is 10M km²!"** → Only with the Antarctic claim, which most countries don't recognise. Without it, about 8.5M. France's is about 10.7M.
- **"The sea circles are wrong"** → They're an illustration: circle area is proportional to each territory's sea area, not the real shape.
- **"Rigged!"** → It was 4–4 before the ocean 🌊
