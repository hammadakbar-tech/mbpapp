## Fact notes
- **Canada:** 9.98M km²; ~41.5M people; coastline 202,080 km (CIA), the longest on Earth; 52,455 islands (NRCan); ~62% of the world's lakes larger than ~10 ha (often cited); ~20% of the world's fresh water (often cited); ~70% of the world's maple syrup.
- **Heat:** Lytton, BC 49.6°C (2021). India: Phalodi 51.0°C (2016).
- **Basketball:** invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a Canadian (at Springfield, Massachusetts).
- **India cricket:** 2 ODI World Cups + 3 T20 World Cups = 5.
- **Mountains:** K2 8,611 m; Mount Logan 5,959 m.
- **Ulaanbaatar:** the coldest national capital. **Mongolia:** more horses than people.
- **Changi:** many-time Skytrax World's Best Airport. **Bangkok:** often ranked the world's most visited city (Mastercard index).
- **Vietnam:** the world's #2 coffee producer.
- Population and area figures: CIA, UN and national statistics (rounded).
