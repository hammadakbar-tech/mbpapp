# Countryballs: Australia vs Japan, the Geography War

**File:** `australia-vs-japan.mp4` (1080×1920, 2:27, AI voices)
**Cover:** `cover.png`

Same multi-shot editing as Canada vs Japan: every line cuts between a close-up, low angle, over-the-shoulder and two-shot, then cuts away to whatever is being talked about, with a reaction shot after each round. Australia talks in an outback sunset with Uluru-style red rock and gum trees. Japan keeps its red sun, Fuji and torii set.

## Ready replies

- **"Vending machines isn't geography!"** → It's geography of fun 😄 About 3.9 million in a country smaller than New South Wales and Victoria combined.
- **"Australia has fast trains"** → Not yet. The fastest regular services run at about 160 km/h. High-speed rail is still in planning.
- **"The ocean round is unfair"** → Exclusive economic zones are real territory under the law of the sea! Australia: over 10M km² (with its external territories). Japan: 4.47M km².
- **"The sea zones in the video look wrong"** → They're an illustration: a 200-nautical-mile band around the main islands only. The official figures include remote islands and territories.
- **"Tokyo bigger than Australia?"** → By people, yes. Tokyo plus 3 neighbouring prefectures ≈ 37M vs Australia ≈ 28M.
- **"Japan's record is higher now"** → The video uses the JMA record of 41.8°C (Isesaki, Aug 2025). If it has been broken since, tell us!
- **"Rigged!"** → It was 4–4 before the ocean round 🌊
