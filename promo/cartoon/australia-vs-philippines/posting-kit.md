|---|---|---|

| Filipino roots | 408,836 people with Filipino ancestry | ABS 2021 Census |
| People | Australia ~28M. Philippines ~112.7M | ABS 2025. PSA 2024 census |
| Islands | Philippines 7,641. Australia 8,222 | NAMRIA (2016). Geoscience Australia |
| Volcanoes | 24 active volcanoes in the Philippines. None on mainland Australia | PHIVOLCS. Heard Island's Big Ben is active but is an external territory, hence "mainland" |
| Mayon | Famous for its near-perfect cone | PHIVOLCS / UNESCO tentative list |
| Reefs | Tubbataha UNESCO (1993). Great Barrier Reef UNESCO (1981), ~2,300 km, the world's largest coral reef system | UNESCO, GBRMPA |
| Christmas | The "ber months": celebrations start in September, often called the world's longest Christmas season | Widely reported tradition |
| Heat | Philippines 42.2°C (Tuguegarao, 1969). Australia 50.7°C (Oodnadatta, 1960) | PAGASA, BoM |
