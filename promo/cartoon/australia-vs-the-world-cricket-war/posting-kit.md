# Countryballs: Australia vs the World, the Cricket War

**File:** `australia-vs-the-world-cricket-war.mp4` (1080×1920, 1:58, AI voices)
**Cover:** `cover.png`

A cricket-only spin-off. Australia takes on 8 cricket nations, one per round: England, India, West Indies, Pakistan, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan and South Africa. Every round is a real record, and winners alternate so it's fair. It ends **4–4**, and viewers pick who Australia plays in the "final" (the next episode), then the subscribe button gets clicked. New Zealand appears as a silver-fern ball, so it doesn't look like Australia.

## Notes

- Voices: announcer US English. Australia, England, India, NZ and South Africa use local English voices. Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Afghanistan use Urdu, Sinhala and Pashto voices reading English. The West Indies uses a British voice, because no Caribbean voice is available. All AI.
- The West Indies and New Zealand balls use custom cricket emblems (a palm and stumps, and a silver fern) instead of national flags.
- Rebuild: `python3 cricket.py <data_dir> out.mp4` (needs the shared promo modules and the flags in `flags/`).
