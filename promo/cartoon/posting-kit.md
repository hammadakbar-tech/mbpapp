## Fact check
- **Size:** total area, including water: Canada about 9.98 million km², USA about 9.83 million km². By land area alone the USA is slightly larger, which is the joke in the USA's "only because you count all your lakes!"
- **Lakes:** Canada holds about 62% of the world's lakes larger than 10 hectares (HydroLAKES, Messager et al. 2016), so more than the rest of the world combined. The card rounds this to "~60%".
- **Population:** USA about 340 million (2024), Canada about 41 million (2025).
- **Moon:** 12 astronauts walked on the Moon (Apollo 11–17, 1969–1972), all Americans.
- **Canadarm:** the Space Shuttle's robotic arm was built in Canada (Spar Aerospace) and first flew in 1981.
- **Economy:** the USA has the world's largest economy by nominal GDP.
- **Basketball:** invented in December 1891 by James Naismith, a Canadian born in Almonte, Ontario, at the YMCA training school in Springfield, Massachusetts.
- **Olympics:** the USA has won the most Olympic gold medals overall. Canada has won the most Olympic ice hockey golds (men's and women's combined).
- **Insulin:** discovered in 1921–22 at the University of Toronto (Banting, Best, Macleod and Collip; Nobel Prize 1923).
- **Internet:** it grew out of ARPANET (US Department of Defense, 1969) and TCP/IP (Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn). The World Wide Web came later (Tim Berners-Lee, CERN, 1989).
- **Border:** about 8,891 km, the longest international border between two countries.
- **Tone:** the characters' boasting ("Obviously me") is played for laughs. Every claim on a fact card is accurate.
