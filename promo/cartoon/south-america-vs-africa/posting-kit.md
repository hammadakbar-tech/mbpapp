## Ready replies

- **"Africa has 55 countries!"** → The African Union has 55 members because it includes Western Sahara. The UN recognises 54, which is the number we used. 🙌
- **"The Amazon is actually longer than the Nile"** → Some studies say so! It depends where you put the source. That's why Brazil says "Longest, maybe" and wins on water instead. 💧
- **"French Guiana is missing"** → It's on the map in grey: it is part of France, not an independent country, so no ball.
- **"The Atacama isn't the driest place"** → Correct, Antarctica's Dry Valleys are drier. The Atacama is the driest non-polar desert.
- **"GDP isn't fair, Africa has more people"** → True, and per person the gap is even bigger. We used total nominal GDP.
- **"Rigged!"** → It ended 4–4. Tell us which round you would change. 😄
- **"Do Asia next"** → On the list. Asia vs who?

## Fact check

| Claim in video | Source value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| One land ~140 million years ago | South Atlantic rifting began about 140 Ma; fully separated about 100 Ma | "Split apart" describes the start of the break-up |
| 54 vs 12 countries | UN member states | AU counts 55 |
| Aconcagua 6,961 m, Kilimanjaro 5,895 m | Standard survey figures | |
| 440 million vs 1.5 billion people | UN 2025 estimates: about 438 million and 1.55 billion | Rounded |
| Nigeria alone: 238 million | UN 2025 estimate, about 237.5 million | More than half of 440 million |
| Nile ~6,650 km, "they call it the longest" | Traditional figure | Disputed with the Amazon; the line is worded to allow for it |
| Amazon carries more water than the next seven rivers combined | Average discharge about 209,000 m³/s | Widely cited comparison |
| Atacama driest desert | Driest non-polar desert | |
| Sahara 9.2 million km² vs Brazil 8.5 million km² | Standard figures | Map overlay is drawn at true scale |
| World Cups 10–0 | Brazil 5, Argentina 3, Uruguay 2; no African winner | Checked against the 2026 tournament: Spain beat Argentina in the final, so the count is unchanged |
| Morocco semi-final | 2022, finished fourth | Still Africa's best result |
| 17.8 vs 30.4 million km² | Standard figures | The 12.5 million km² difference is larger than the USA (9.8 million) |
| GDP: Africa ~$2.8T, Brazil ~$2.2T, South America over $4T | IMF nominal GDP, 2024–25 | These move every year with exchange rates; the least stable numbers in the video |
