# Countryballs: UK vs USA, the Rematch

**File:** `uk-vs-usa-the-rematch.mp4` (1080×1920, 2:00, AI voices)
**Cover:** `cover.png`

Made for posting while Australia sleeps, when the UK, Europe and the US are awake. The hook is the Boston Tea Party: "250 years later, it's rematch time." Eight fair rounds that alternate winners. It ends **4–4 on purpose**: viewers pick the tiebreaker, then the subscribe button gets clicked.

| People | UK ~69M (ONS mid-2024). USA ~342M (Census Bureau) | |
