## Fact check

| Claim | Value used | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Islands | Philippines 7,641 (NAMRIA 2016). Indonesia 17,000+ (17,380 named islands, 2023 national gazetteer) | Older Indonesian estimates were ~17,500. "About 25,000 together" uses these two counts |
| FIBA crowd | 38,115 at Philippine Arena, Aug 25, 2023 (Philippines vs Dominican Republic). It beat the 1994 Toronto record (32,616). The Philippines, Japan and Indonesia co-hosted | FIBA, Inquirer, ESPN |
| People | Philippines ~112.7M (2024 census). Indonesia ~284M (2025), the 4th most populous country | PSA, BPS / UN |
| Eagles | Philippine eagle ~86–102 cm long, wingspan ~1.8–2.2 m, among the largest eagles. Javan hawk-eagle ~56–61 cm | |
| Volcanoes | Philippines 24 active (PHIVOLCS). Indonesia ~127 active (PVMBG), the most of any country | Counts vary by definition |
| Pearls | Indonesia is the largest producer of South Sea pearls. The Pearl of Puerto Princesa: ~34 kg, ~67 cm, found off Palawan, widely reported as the largest ever | That pearl hasn't been independently certified as a gem pearl |
| Tarsier | Each eyeball is roughly as big as its brain | Widely cited |
| Komodo dragon | The largest living lizard, up to ~3 m, ~70 kg | |
| Social media | ~3.5 hours a day, consistently top 5 worldwide | DataReportal / Meltwater Digital 2025 (survey estimate) |
