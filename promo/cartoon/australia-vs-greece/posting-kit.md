## Ready replies

- **"Australia has more islands than Greece?!"** → Yes! 8,222 vs about 6,000 (only ~227 are inhabited in Greece). Most of Australia's are tiny and uninhabited 🏝️
- **"Melbourne is the 3rd biggest Greek city!"** → It's a famous claim! Officially Victoria has about 181,000 people with Greek ancestry, one of the biggest Greek communities outside Greece 🇬🇷
- **"Greece's heat record is higher"** → Greece's widely cited record is 48.0°C (Athens, 1977). Australia's is 50.7°C.
- **"Olive oil: Spain/Italy use more"** → Greece is at or near the top per person among the big olive oil countries. Figures vary by year.
- **"Sheep numbers changed"** → Australia's flock was ~74M in June 2025. Greece ~6.4–7.8M depending on the survey.
- **"Rigged!"** → It was 4–4… and you can't beat Greece at being Greek 😂
