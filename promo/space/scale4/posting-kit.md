## Fact check (all rounded)
- **"A million billion times":** a person is about 1.7 m tall and a proton about 1.7 femtometres (1.7 × 10⁻¹⁵ m) across. The ratio is 10¹⁵, a million billion (one quadrillion).
- **30 trillion cells:** about 3 × 10¹³ human cells for a reference adult (Sender, Fuchs & Milo 2016). A 2023 study (Hatton et al., *PNAS*) estimated about 36 trillion for men and 28 trillion for women. Bacteria aren't counted.
- **Cell and nucleus size:** human cells range from about 5 µm to over 100 µm, and "about ten micrometres" is a typical figure. An average mammalian nucleus is about 6 µm across.
- **2 metres of DNA per cell:** each cell has about 6.4 billion base pairs (two copies of the genome) × 0.34 nm per pair, which comes to about 2.2 m.
- **DNA:** the double helix is about 2 nm wide, with about 10 base pairs per 3.4 nm turn. One copy of the human genome is about 3.1 billion base pairs, the "about three billion letters".
- **Atom size:** atoms are about 0.1–0.5 nm across (hydrogen about 0.1 nm). "Roughly a ten-billionth of a metre" is the textbook order of magnitude.
- **Empty space:** a nucleus is about 1/10,000 to 1/100,000 the width of its atom, so by volume about 99.9999999999996% of an atom lies outside the nucleus. This is the classical picture; in quantum mechanics that space is filled by the electrons' probability cloud.
- **Stadium and rice:** for a hydrogen atom (about 0.1 nm) and a proton (about 1.7 fm) the ratio is about 1 : 60,000. Scale the atom to a 200 m stadium and the nucleus is about 3 mm, a grain of rice.
- **Quarks:** a proton holds two up quarks and one down quark, bound by gluons. The strong force is the strongest of the four fundamental forces. The flickering dots in the proton are the "sea" of short-lived quark–antiquark pairs. Quark "colours" are a name for their strong-force charge.
- **"No one knows":** quarks appear point-like in every experiment so far (no size detected down to about 10⁻¹⁸ to 10⁻¹⁹ m). Whether anything is smaller is an open question.
- **Visuals:** the zoom and the screen-width ruler are roughly to scale. The hologram figure, fingerprint whorl, cell mosaic and organelles are illustrations. So are the DNA (a 2D projection with true width and turn length), the atoms (drawn as spheres, then clouds) and the carbon-12 nucleus (6 protons, 6 neutrons). Real skin's outer layer is made of dead flattened cells; living cells with nuclei sit just beneath.

Sources: Sender, Fuchs & Milo 2016 (*PLOS Biology*); Hatton et al. 2023 (*PNAS*); NIH National Human Genome Research Institute (genome size, DNA); CODATA proton charge radius (about 0.84 fm); CERN on quarks and the strong force.
