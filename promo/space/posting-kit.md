## Fact check (all rounded)
- **Venus:** surface about 465 °C; pressure about 92× Earth's; clouds of sulfuric acid; one rotation (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 days).
- **Mars:** average about −63 °C; atmosphere under 1% of Earth's pressure; global dust storms; blue sunsets (seen by NASA rovers).
- **Titan:** about −179 °C; lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane; gravity 14% of Earth's; air 1.5× Earth's pressure (human-powered flight would be possible).
- **Europa:** ice shell many kilometres thick over an ocean with roughly twice Earth's ocean water; surface radiation about 5.4 Sv/day (a lethal dose within about a day).

Sources: NASA Solar System Exploration (science.nasa.gov) planet and moon fact pages.
