## Fact check
- **TON 618:** an ultramassive black hole powering a quasar in the constellation Canes Venatici. Mass estimates range from about 40 billion (2019) to about 66 billion (2004) suns. The video says "about 40 billion", the conservative figure.
- **"20+ times":** at 40 billion solar masses the event horizon is roughly 1,600 AU across. Neptune's orbit is about 60 AU across, so that's about 26 times, and more under the higher mass estimate.
- **Light travel time:** about 10.8 billion years. Earth is about 4.5 billion years old, so the light set off before Earth formed.
- **The image:** an artist's-style rendering (accretion disk, lensed halo, photon ring). No telescope can resolve TON 618 this closely.

Sources: Wikipedia "TON 618" (citing Ge et al. 2019 and Shemmer et al. 2004); NASA on black holes and event horizons.
