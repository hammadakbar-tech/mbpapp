## Reply ideas

- **"Bigger than India??"** → *Its ocean territory (exclusive economic zone) is about 3.4M km²; India's land is about 3.3M km². Its actual land is only ~811 km².*
- **"Is it really going to disappear?"** → *Most land is only a few metres above sea level. Scientists warn that flooding and salt water could make parts unlivable this century, but it depends on how much seas rise.*

## Fact check
- **Islands:** 33 islands (32 atolls plus raised Banaba), in three groups: Gilbert, Phoenix and Line Islands. From Banaba to Millennium (Caroline) Island is over 4,000 km.
- **Land vs water:**
  - Land is about 811 km², smaller than Hong Kong (about 1,106 km²).
  - The exclusive economic zone is about 3.44 million km², larger than India's land area (about 3.29 million km²).
  - The three zone circles in the video are equal-area stand-ins, not exact boundaries.
- **Hemispheres:** Kiribati straddles both the equator and the 180th meridian, which makes it the only country in all four hemispheres.
- **Distances:** Kiritimati to Hawaii is about 2,000 km; Kiritimati to Tarawa is about 3,300 km.
- **Date line:**
  - On 1 January 1995, Kiribati moved its eastern islands to the same day as the west.
  - The Line Islands (UTC+14) are the first inhabited place to reach each new day and new year.
  - The date-line shape in the video is approximate.
- **Kiritimati:** the largest coral atoll by land area (about 388 km²).
- **History:**
  - Austronesian/Micronesian voyagers settled the islands well before European contact.
  - They became the British Gilbert Islands.
  - The Battle of Tarawa was fought in November 1943.
  - Independence came on 12 July 1979.
  - "Kiribati" is the Gilbertese rendering of "Gilberts" (ti = "s").
- **Sea level:** most land is only about 2–3 m above sea level; flooding and salt water entering freshwater lenses are documented problems.
- **Fiji land:** in 2014 Kiribati bought about 20 km² (roughly 6,000 acres) on Vanua Levu, Fiji, for food security. Fiji is about 2,000 km from Tarawa.
- **Population:**
  - About 120,000 at the 2020 census; about 130,000 today (estimate).
  - South Tarawa had about 63,000, roughly half.
