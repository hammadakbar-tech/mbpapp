**How it plays:**
- **Hook (fast cuts):**
  1. "WALK ACROSS A WHOLE COUNTRY": a walker crosses Vatican City at true scale while a minute timer runs.
  2. "SMALLER THAN CENTRAL PARK": Monaco next to Central Park.
  3. "NO CAPITAL": Nauru with a big ❓.
  4. All ten shapes pop in: "THE 10 SMALLEST COUNTRIES ON EARTH".
- **Countdown #10 → #1:** each country is shown at **true size** next to Manhattan (or Central Park for #2 and #1), with a scale bar, a locator globe, its area and two fact chips.
  - Island nations (Maldives, Marshall Islands, Tuvalu) show how far their islands are spread.
  - A progress row at the bottom builds anticipation for #1.
- **CTA:** "Which one would you visit?" Every viewer has a favourite.

## Reply ideas
- **"What about Sealand?"** → *Sealand is a "micronation" on an old sea fort. It isn't recognised by any country, so it's not counted.*
- **"Monaco looks bigger in other maps"** → *Many maps include its sea area. Its land is only about 2 km², smaller than Central Park (3.4 km²).*
- **"Is the Vatican really a country?"** → *Yes! It's been an independent state since the 1929 Lateran Treaty, with its own post office, bank and the Swiss Guard.*

## Fact check
- **Areas (land, CIA World Factbook):**

  | Country | Area |
  |---|---|
  | Vatican City | 0.44 km² (OSM outline 0.49 km²) |
  | Monaco | 2.02 km² |
  | Nauru | 21 km² |
  | Tuvalu | 26 km² |
  | San Marino | 61 km² |
  | Liechtenstein | 160 km² |
  | Marshall Islands | 181 km² |
  | St Kitts & Nevis | 261 km² |
  | Maldives | 298 km² |
  | Malta | 316 km² |

  Manhattan is about 59 km²; Central Park is 3.41 km².
- **Outlines:**
  - Vatican, San Marino, Liechtenstein and Nauru use OpenStreetMap outlines.
  - Monaco's land strip is traced and scaled to exactly 2.02 km² (official outlines include its sea).
  - The others use Natural Earth 1:10m; small islands are simplified.
  - Manhattan's outline is simplified.
- **Walk:** Vatican City is about 1 km across, so a walk of roughly 15–20 minutes.
- **Malta:** the smallest EU member; about 560,000 people (2023).
- **Maldives:** about 1,190 coral islands; highest natural point about 2.4 m, the lowest of any country.
- **St Kitts & Nevis:** the smallest country in the Americas by both area and population.
- **Marshall Islands:** 29 atolls and 5 islands; the US nuclear tests at Bikini Atoll ran from 1946 to 1958.
- **Liechtenstein:** one of only two doubly landlocked countries (with Uzbekistan); it abolished its army in 1868.
- **San Marino:** by tradition founded in 301 AD; considered the world's oldest surviving republic.
- **Tuvalu:** the ".tv" domain is leased out, and its income makes up a significant share of government revenue.
- **Nauru:** a ring road of about 19 km goes around the island; there is no official capital (government offices are in Yaren).
- **Monaco:** about 38,000 people on about 2 km², the highest population density of any country.
- **Vatican:** about 800 residents; its army is the Pontifical Swiss Guard.
